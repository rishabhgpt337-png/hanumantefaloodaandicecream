Humari Kahani (Our Brand Story)

Starting our sweet journey 26 years ago on the vibrant streets of Mulund West, Hanumante Special Faluda Ice Cream has grown into a legendary dessert destination. Named in honor of Bajrang Bali, our work is our worship, and we serve each customer with devotional warmth and a sweet smile.

We make our own signature slow-cooked saffron syrups, rich cardamom rabri, and silky smooth cornstarch vermicelli daily to ensure pure taste. Every single glass represents 26 years of culinary perfection, family values, and authentic street-food legacy.

Legendary Rabri

Made by boiling full-cream milk slowly for hours. Pure creaminess, no artificial thickening.

Quality & Hygiene

We use premium dry fruits, branded fresh milk, and prepare items in sterile conditions.
